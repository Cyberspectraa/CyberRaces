# Third-party attribution

## Icarus

CyberRaces integrates with Icarus by Cammie / Up-Mods for wing items, flight behaviour and the Zanza's Wings model/resources.

Source: https://github.com/Up-Mods/Icarus

CyberRaces does not bundle Icarus assets. Icarus is a required runtime dependency for the current Fairy racial-wing implementation.
